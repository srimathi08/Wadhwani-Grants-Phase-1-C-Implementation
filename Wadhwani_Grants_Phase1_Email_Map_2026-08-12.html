How to read this

This page answers one question: when the platform moves an application from one state to the next, who gets told. Every row is one state change. Read across a row to see everyone who hears about that moment. Read down a column to see one person’s whole inbox, start to finish.

Four people use the platform, and each needs different things said to them:

  • Partner - the organisation applying for a grant. Called the applicant on screen.
  • Validator - the staff member who checks a submitted application for missing pieces. Judges nothing else.
  • Reviewer - the staff member who evaluates the application and recommends approval or decline.
  • Approver - the person who takes the funding decision.

The state names in the left column are the ones the applicant sees on screen. The flag numbers beside them are the internal identifiers from the Applicant Status Journey Spec v1.0. Solid boxes exist in the platform today. Dashed boxes do not fire at all.

Flag 4 waits on a person nobody notifies

The approver is the only person with no configured email at all. E-22 is what tells them a recommendation is waiting. Until it fires, Approval in Progress is a state the platform enters and never leaves on its own. Nothing else recovers it.

The onboarding path is gated on one unconfigured email

E-12 is the reviewer’s compliance request, and it is the only thing that moves an approved applicant into Onboarding initiated. Nothing after it can happen until it fires. That leaves the whole onboarding path, 5 further state changes, unreachable today.

Nothing counts the rounds

A third return reads exactly like a first, for the applicant and for the validator. E-04 and E-28 carry no round number, and E-17 does not tell a validator whether an application is new or coming back. If repeat returns matter, the field that would carry it does not exist.

Build in this order

Given below are 6 items, ordered by what a build should tackle first:

  • The approver is invisible to the platform - 4 emails, none configured. The one person whose decision the Approval in Progress state waits on is never told anything, never chased, and never sees the reviewer’s reasoning. Configuring E-22 alone unblocks the decision stage.
  • E-10 sends a decision with no reason - configured, live, and the reason panel has nothing bound to it. This reaches applicants today.
  • The applicant journey ends at approval - 9 of their 20 emails are configured and all 9 sit before Application Approved. E-12 is the gate: nothing in onboarding can start until it fires.
  • The internal return loop has no notifications at all - E-46, E-48, E-49, E-51 and E-31 are all built and all unconfigured. Work passes between validator, reviewer and approver with nothing announcing it.
  • Nothing ever closes - the day-100 machinery, E-53, E-59 and E-60, is entirely unconfigured. An abandoned application stays open and holds a place in a queue.
  • The only reminders that work are the ones chasing staff - E-07 and E-18 are configured. Every reminder aimed at an applicant, E-52 and E-56, or at an approver, E-55, is not.

Open questions

Each one blocks a specific configuration. None of them is a preference:

  • Which copy does E-06 carry - the E-06 and E-07 screenshots are word for word identical. Either E-06 is configured with the deadline reminder, or a screenshot was pasted twice. Quadrobay to answer.
  • Does a compliance document get a second try - E-24 says yes, the specification says no. WCF to decide.
  • Ninety days or one hundred - the v8 copy for E-52 and E-53 says 90. The specification says the timer runs to 100. The content reference now says 100 throughout. WCF to confirm.
  • What triggers E-10 on a compliance rejection - the specification routes it there and the wiring is unconfirmed. Quadrobay to confirm.
  • {{decision_due_date}} does not exist in the platform - the date the approver decision window closes is not built today, and four approver emails, E-22, E-47, E-50 and E-55, all name it. Owner: Quadrobay.
  • 18 merge fields carry no description - a merge field is a placeholder the platform fills in as it sends. For 18 of them the content reference records only “not yet described, confirm what this holds before build,” so nobody has confirmed what they hold. All 18 are listed in the field register in the content reference.
  • The duplicate-match screen for E-58 is to be built - match on legal name, country, and legal representative or founder name. Owner: Quadrobay.
  • Is E-11 switched off - it is live today and tells an applicant that a decision they never received is under review.
  • Subject lines and preheaders are unverified - the preheader is the hidden line the inbox shows after the subject. Neither appears in a rendered screenshot. Nothing on this page covers what is live.
  • Do the compliance states exist in the build - Compliance Review, Onboarding Approved and Onboarded need compliance review, compliance approval and agreement execution to exist as states. Unconfirmed.
  • When does a decision email fire - at the 7-day window expiry, or on a silent close. This changes what the applicant sees and when.
